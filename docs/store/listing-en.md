# kobiPDF — Microsoft Store listing (English, en-US)

## Product name
kobiPDF

## Short description
A free PDF reader and editor for small businesses. Open PDFs, fix text, add signatures and stamps, organize pages — offline, entirely on your PC.

## Description
kobiPDF is a free PDF reader and editor, a gift from Hidroteknik to small businesses.

Handle everyday PDF work in one app: correct a date in a quotation, sign a contract and add your company stamp, highlight key lines on an invoice, delete pages you don't need, or merge several PDFs into one file.

PDF viewer
• Fast opening, page thumbnails and bookmarks
• Continuous or single-page view, fit to page or width
• Open several PDFs in tabs
• Print preview

PDF editor
• Fix existing text in the document; the original font and line are kept, and vertical text stays vertical
• Add new text and images
• Highlight, underline, strikeout, pen, eraser, sticky notes and comment boxes
• Draw a signature or upload a signature/stamp image; saved signatures are ready next time
• Create a new PDF from scratch: blank A4, A5 or any custom size

Page tools
• Rotate, delete and drag to reorder pages
• Extract pages to a new file, merge and split PDFs

Private and simple
• No internet needed; your documents are never uploaded
• No ads, subscriptions or accounts
• Turkish and English interface
• Open source (AGPL-3.0): github.com/alperalyaz/kobiPDF
