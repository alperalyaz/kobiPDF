## Full description (max 4000)
kobiPDF is a PDF reader and editor made by Hidroteknik for small businesses.

Correct a date in a quotation that just arrived on your phone, add your signature and company stamp to a contract,
then send it back through your messaging or e-mail app with one tap.

Open the PDF
• Open it straight from file manager, messaging and e-mail apps with "Open with" or "Share"
• Smooth scrolling and pinch-to-zoom, even in large documents

Fix the text
• Tap the text you want to change and type the new one
• The old text is really removed from the document; the new one sits in the same place and at the same size
• Line length stays exactly the same in documents written in Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, Courier New and Georgia
• You can also add new text to the page

Signature and stamp
• Draw your signature with your finger or upload a stamp or signature image
• Move and resize it with your finger
• Signatures you used are ready next time

Save and share
• The edited document is saved to Documents/kobiPDF without touching the original
• Send it to any app with the Share button

Private and simple
• No internet needed; your documents are never uploaded
• No ads, subscriptions or accounts
• Turkish and English interface
• Open source (AGPL-3.0): github.com/alperalyaz/kobiPDF
